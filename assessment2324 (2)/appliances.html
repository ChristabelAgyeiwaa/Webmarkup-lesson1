Kitchen Appliances

Kitchen appliances can make cooking faster and easier. Knowing what they do helps you choose the right one.

Oven

An oven uses hot air to cook food evenly. It is commonly used for baking bread and cakes, roasting vegetables, and cooking meat. Always use oven gloves when handling hot trays or dishes.

Microwave

A microwave is useful for quickly heating and reheating food. It can also cook simple foods such as vegetables and potatoes. Always use microwave-safe containers and avoid putting metal inside.

Air Fryer

An air fryer cooks food by circulating hot air around it. It can make foods such as potatoes, vegetables and chicken crispy while using less oil than traditional frying. An air fryer can also be a useful option for students or people who do not have access to an oven.

Blender

A blender uses fast-moving blades to mix ingredients until they are smooth. It is useful for making smoothies, soups, sauces and other blended foods. Make sure the lid is secure before turning it on.
