Easy Recipes for Beginners

Put your new cooking skills into practice with simple recipes and easy-to-follow steps. These meals use everyday ingredients and are suitable for beginner cooks.

Greek Chicken Bowl

Cooking time: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

This Greek-inspired chicken bowl combines seasoned chicken, roasted potatoes, fresh vegetables, feta cheese and creamy tzatziki for a filling and colourful meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 200 g baby potatoes
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 50 g feta cheese
  • 2 tablespoons tzatziki
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into small pieces and season them with olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast the potatoes until golden and tender.
  3. Season the chicken with oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Cook the chicken in a frying pan until fully cooked, then slice it into pieces.
  5. Chop the cucumber, tomato and red onion.
  6. Add the potatoes, chicken and vegetables to a bowl.
  7. Finish with crumbled feta and tzatziki on top.

Teriyaki Salmon Bowl

Cooking time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

This colourful salmon bowl combines teriyaki-glazed salmon, rice, edamame, avocado and a fresh mango-pineapple salsa. It is a simple way to create a filling meal in one bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 100 g rice
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 50 g edamame
  • 1/2 mango
  • 2 pineapple slices
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 1 spring onion

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to the instructions on the package.
  2. Brush the salmon with teriyaki sauce.
  3. Cook the salmon in a frying pan until it is cooked through and flakes easily.
  4. Cook the edamame and slice the avocado.
  5. Dice the mango, pineapple and red onion, then mix them together to make the salsa.
  6. Add the cooked rice to a bowl and arrange the salmon, edamame, avocado and salsa on top.
  7. Finish with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.

Steak with Chimichurri & Potato Wedges

Cooking time: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

This meal combines sliced steak with crispy potato wedges, fresh chimichurri and a colourful avocado and corn salsa. It is a great recipe for practising how to cook steak and prepare simple homemade sides.

Ingredients

  • 1 steak
  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 50 g sweetcorn
  • 1 small tomato
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Fresh parsley
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into wedges and season them with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Bake the wedges until they are golden and tender.
  3. Finely chop the parsley and garlic and mix them with a little olive oil to make the chimichurri.
  4. Mix the avocado, sweetcorn, tomato and red onion together to make the salsa.
  5. Season the steak with salt and pepper.
  6. Cook the steak in a hot frying pan to your preferred doneness and allow it to rest before slicing.
  7. Serve the sliced steak with the chimichurri, potato wedges and salsa.

Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta

Cooking time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

This creamy pasta combines seasoned chicken with a rich garlic and Parmesan sauce. It is a comforting beginner-friendly meal that can be prepared using simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 100 g pasta
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100 ml cooking cream
  • 30 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian herbs
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the instructions on the package.
  2. Season the chicken with Italian herbs, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and cook the chicken until fully cooked.
  4. Remove the chicken from the pan and allow it to rest before slicing.
  5. Add the garlic to the same pan and cook briefly.
  6. Add the cooking cream and Parmesan, then stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
  7. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix well.
  8. Serve with the sliced chicken on top and finish with fresh parsley.

Creamy Garlic Chicken with Mashed Potatoes & Carrots

Cooking time: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

This comforting meal combines tender chicken with a creamy garlic sauce, smooth mashed potatoes and roasted carrots. It is a simple way to practise preparing several parts of a meal at the same time.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100 ml cooking cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and cut the potatoes, then boil them until soft.
  2. Cut the carrots into pieces, season with olive oil, salt and pepper, and roast until tender.
  3. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook the chicken in a frying pan until fully cooked, then remove it from the pan.
  5. Mash the cooked potatoes with butter until smooth.
  6. Add the garlic to the frying pan and cook briefly.
  7. Add the cooking cream and stir gently to create the sauce.
  8. Return the chicken to the pan and coat it with the creamy garlic sauce.
  9. Serve with the mashed potatoes and roasted carrots, then finish with fresh parsley.
