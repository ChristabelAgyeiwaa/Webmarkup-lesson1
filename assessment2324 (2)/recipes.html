Easy Recipes for Beginners

Put your new cooking skills into practice with simple recipes and easy-to-follow steps. These meals use everyday ingredients and are suitable for beginner cooks.

Protein

Protein is an important nutrient that helps the body grow, build muscles and repair tissues. Good sources of protein include chicken, fish, eggs, beans and lentils. For beginners, simple meals such as eggs on toast, chicken with rice, or lentil soup are easy ways to include protein. Adding protein to a meal can also help you feel full for longer.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are one of the body's main sources of energy. Foods such as rice, pasta, potatoes, bread and oats contain carbohydrates. They are useful for beginner cooks because they are affordable, easy to prepare and can be combined with many other ingredients. For example, rice can be served with vegetables and chicken to create a simple meal.

Fruits & Vegetables

Fruits and vegetables provide important vitamins, minerals and fibre that are part of a balanced diet. Eating different types and colours gives your body a variety of nutrients. Fresh, frozen and canned options can all be useful when preparing meals.

Beginners can easily add vegetables to pasta, rice, wraps, soups and stir-fries. Fruits can be eaten as snacks or added to breakfast, smoothies and desserts.

Healthy Fats

Healthy fats provide energy and help the body absorb certain vitamins. Foods such as avocado, nuts, seeds and olive oil are common sources of healthy fats.

They can easily be added to everyday meals. For example, you can use olive oil when cooking vegetables, add avocado to a wrap or include nuts and seeds in breakfast meals.

Building a Balanced Meal

A balanced meal can include a source of protein, carbohydrates, fruits or vegetables and a small amount of healthy fat. Combining different food groups helps create meals that provide a variety of nutrients.

For example, a simple balanced meal could include chicken, rice, mixed vegetables and a small amount of olive oil. You do not need to make every meal perfect. Learning how to combine different food groups is a good place to start.
